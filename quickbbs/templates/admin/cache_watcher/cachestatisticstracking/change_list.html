How to read Sizing Advice

A low hit rate has two unrelated causes, and only one of them means the cache should actually grow:

  • Eviction pressure — the cache is running near full (current_size close to max_size) and the hit rate is low. Entries are being evicted before they get reused. This is the case where raising the corresponding *_CACHE_SIZE setting in quickbbs_settings.py should help.
  • Cold-key traffic — the hit rate is low but current_size is well below max_size. The cache has room to spare; the misses aren't from eviction, they're from a workload that's inherently one-shot (e.g. a bulk scan touching thousands of distinct files once each). No max_size increase fixes this — the same key is never asked for twice.
  • Healthy, oversized — the hit rate is high but current_size stays well below max_size. The cache could likely be shrunk without losing hits, freeing memory for something else.

The advice column is a hint, not a verdict — it can't see your actual traffic pattern, only one snapshot's numbers. Treat "consider raising" or "could shrink" as a starting point for a decision, not an instruction, and watch several snapshots (via settings.CACHE_MONITORING = True) before changing a size. See quickbbs_app_design.md §4.5 for the full reasoning.
